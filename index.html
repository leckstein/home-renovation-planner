Tile format decision

1200 × 1200 — Nyra Hay

Most minimal/premium. Fewest grout lines, but harder set-out and more cutting on short walls.

600 × 1200 — practical challenger

Best alternative format. Still large-format but easier around vanities, bath and shorter walls. Compare CTM Ravine Rock Beige and Italtile Wembley Bone directly against Nyra.

Decision rule: if a 600×1200 option is at least 90% as attractive as Nyra in person, I would favour 600×1200 for easier execution. If Nyra is noticeably better, keep Nyra.
