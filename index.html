Bathrooms

Shared shower

1200 × 950 mm. Preferred enclosure is custom frameless corner-entry with two pivot/hinged doors meeting at the corner, rather than sliders. Use brushed/satin stainless hardware where possible.

Full-height Nyra Hay; Qube only as the required top infill flush to ceiling. Shower floor is Sliced Interlocking Beige, Tan & Cream Pebble Mosaic with light-grey grout.

Ensuite shower

Approx. 1900 × 1200 × 2450 mm; depth increased to 1200 mm to align with a full Nyra Hay tile. No window in the shower. Full-height Nyra Hay with Qube top infill flush to ceiling.

Small masonry step block in the corner at the entrance, not a bench. Nyra Hay on top; vertical faces may remain Nyra Hay or use Qube as a controlled accent. Reuse existing frameless glass where workable; use the purchased exposed stainless shower column.

Shared bath

Duravit D-Code 1500 × 750 built-in bath purchased. Bath sits between two walls with window behind/above and bath mixer on the side wall.

Bath apron design is being compared: Nyra Hay vs centred Nyra 3D Qube. If Qube is selected, incorporate concealed removable access to waste/trap and confirm hatch opening with plumber before tile setting-out.

Tile setting-out / trim

Outside the full-height shower walls, use a single 1200 mm-high Nyra Hay course where planned, with painted wall above. Pencil trim finishes the exposed horizontal tile-to-paint edge only.

12 × 1.2 m pencil trims = 14.4 m available. Do not use pencil trim automatically at shower ceilings, internal corners, bath apron or step. Qube at the top of showers is flush to the ceiling.
