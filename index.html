Basin mixer choice — what the two routes look like

Ferrara deck-mounted

304 stainlessbrushed / satin look
Preferred

The basin mixer sits on the quartz top behind the basin. It visually matches the Ferrara shower columns and bath mixer, so the room reads as one metal family.

Practical advantage: lower cost and simpler plumbing. Quartz is drilled for the mixer after the vanity position is confirmed.

Gadoni wall-mounted

polished chrome15 yr guarantee
Alternative

The mixer is concealed in the wall and leaves the quartz top completely clear. The look is more architectural and minimal, but it introduces polished chrome next to the brushed Ferrara stainless used elsewhere.

Practical implication: exact basin centre, spout projection and height must be fixed before waterproofing/tiling. Current price is materially higher than Ferrara.
