Flooring finalists

deZIGN S250 Blond Oak

2.5 mm glue-down • 0.55 mm wear layer • Class 33

Working budget R291/m². Stronger wear-layer specification. Retailer warranty wording varies, so obtain the manufacturer warranty certificate applicable to your supplier and installation.

Leno Optimum Mordent Oak

2 mm glue-down • 0.3 mm wear layer

R279/m², 10-year residential / 5-year light-commercial warranty. Grey/white bleached oak. Current Flooring Depot listing has shown stock constraints, so confirm enough of one batch before choosing.

Whole-batch rule

At 330 m² net + 10% spare/cuts, the target order is 363 m². Buy or reserve the full amount from one production batch once the sample is approved.

Visible metal finish

Recommended: stay predominantly brushed/satin stainless. Ferrara is 304 stainless and visually reads softer than polished chrome. Pair it with stainless accessories such as CTM Vittoria/Giulia and satin/silver stair/floor trims.

If you choose the Gadoni wall-mounted basin mixer, note that it is polished chrome. Mixing it directly beside Ferrara stainless may look accidental unless you deliberately repeat chrome elsewhere. For that reason, Ferrara deck-mounted remains the more cohesive default.

Practical stages

1Lock flooring, basin tap route, vanity widths, mirrors and glass approach.
2Measure full upstairs and staircase; reserve one LVT batch including skirting/spares.
3Demolition + plumbing/electrical first-fix. Wall-mounted taps must be fixed before tiling.
4Assess exposed subfloor, specify levelling/primer and waterproof shower zones.
5Tile bathrooms: shower walls to ceiling, remaining walls half-height.
6Site-measure and manufacture vanities after wall build-up/plumbing centres are fixed.
7Install glue-down LVT, stair profiles and matching LVT skirting.
8Final fittings, mirrors, glass, silicone, testing and snagging.
